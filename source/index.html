Our story

A little more personal.
A lot more you.

Royal Icon Furnitures is a South African furniture manufacturing and sales company based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. We specialise in quality, stylish, comfortable and durable couches and customised upholstered furniture, made from scratch to your specifications.

We work with you on sizes, dimensions, designs, styles, colours, fabrics, upholstery, finishes and individual requirements. Personalised service and craftsmanship guide every piece, with value for money at its heart. We aim to serve customers throughout Gauteng and other parts of South Africa; delivery coverage is confirmed for each order.

Our mission

Our mission is to manufacture quality, stylish and durable furniture while providing reliable and personalised service to every customer.

We aim to build a trusted South African furniture brand known for craftsmanship, affordability, customisation and customer satisfaction. We want every customer to feel that their furniture was made specifically for them and that they received value for their money.

Our vision

Our vision is to grow Royal Icon Furnitures into a leading South African furniture manufacturing brand with a strong presence across the country and eventually internationally.

We aim to expand our manufacturing capacity, establish professional showrooms, create employment opportunities, introduce more furniture products and build a brand that customers across South Africa can trust.

Frequently asked questions.

What to know before making a piece your own.

Do you manufacture couches from scratch?

Yes. Royal Icon Furnitures manufactures couches and customised upholstered furniture from scratch according to an agreed customer brief.

Can I customise the size of my couch?

Yes. Share your preferred dimensions and room measurements. We discuss the design and confirm the final specifications before manufacturing.

Can I choose the fabric and colour?

Yes. You can discuss fabrics, upholstery, colours and finishes with us. The swatches on this website are inspiration only; availability and suitability must be confirmed.

Can I request a design that is not displayed?

Yes. Share your ideas or reference photos and we will discuss what can be manufactured. A reference is a starting point; the final design and requirements must be agreed.

Do you make furniture for businesses and BnBs?

Yes. We work with residential and commercial customers, including salons, BnBs, accommodation businesses, entertainment venues, interior decorators and businesses requiring customised furniture.

How do I request a quotation?

Complete the custom furniture form to prepare a readable enquiry. Copy it into a message to our verified Instagram account, or use WhatsApp sharing if you already have our confirmed business contact. Direct WhatsApp routing and email submission await confirmed contact details.

How does the ordering process work?

Enquire, discuss and confirm your specifications, receive and review a quotation, and accept it. Deposit and payment arrangements are then confirmed under the business’s actual terms. Manufacturing follows the agreed process, with updates where applicable, before the finished furniture is prepared and collection or delivery is arranged.

How long does manufacturing take?

Royal Icon Furnitures must confirm the manufacturing lead time for your design and order before you proceed. No fixed turnaround time has been supplied for this website.

Do you offer delivery?

Furniture sales and delivery form part of our service. Tell us your delivery area. Coverage in Gauteng and elsewhere in South Africa, charges, timing and access arrangements must be confirmed with the business for each order. Collection arrangements must also be agreed.

What are the payment and deposit arrangements?

The business must confirm the deposit, accepted payment methods, payment schedule and other applicable terms in your quotation or order agreement. No percentages or payment policies have been supplied here.

How can I contact Royal Icon Furnitures?

Use our verified Instagram account, @royal_icon_furnitures, or prepare an enquiry below. Telephone, WhatsApp number, email address and a full visit or collection address are awaiting owner confirmation; please arrange any visit with the business first.
